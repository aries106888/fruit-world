Did You Know?

  1. Bananas are berries according to botanical classification.
  2. Strawberries have their tiny seeds on the outside.
  3. Pineapples are made up of many smaller fruits joined together.
  4. Apples can float in water because they contain air.
  5. Lemons and limes are known for their sour taste.
  6. Dragon fruit has tiny black seeds inside its soft flesh.
  7. Blueberries can be used to make colourful juices and desserts.
  8. Oranges are popular fruits for making fresh juice.
  9. Some fruits have skins that protect the soft fruit inside.
  10. Jackfruit is one of the largest fruits that grows on a tree.
